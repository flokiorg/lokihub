Release candidate for 0.5.0, covering everything merged to `main` since 0.5.0-rc.6.

## Cash Hub
- The hub page at `/cash-hub/:id` carries hub management again — permissions, hub settings, transactions, balance and top-up, connection details, delete — and the bill list is the full one, so minting, per-bill QR and per-bill delete are back. (#107)
- Reordered around what an operator reaches for: balance and outstanding first, then the bills, then the charts behind a disclosure that remembers whether it was left open. (#107)
- Outstanding no longer counts bills whose redeem window has passed but which the sweep has not yet collected. They are not redeemable and were already counted as returned, so the totals could not add up. (#107)
- The outstanding chart now meets the figure printed above it. It accumulated from zero over a 30-day window, so it missed anything issued earlier, and ignored splits and write-offs entirely. (#107)
- A bill's own page shows the same six-status vocabulary as the hub's list, where it previously showed a coarser one for the same slices. (#107)
- Archived bills no longer offer delete, bulk-select, QR reveal or navigation against a wallet that no longer exists. (#107)
- Every figure on the hub page explains itself, and the status legend shows the badge it describes. (#107)

## Fixed
- Creating a Cash Hub no longer claims its connection can be viewed again later. A hub's pairing secret is random and never stored, unlike a bill's, so the screen that shows it is the only one that ever will. (#107)
- Removing a bill now says the bill is destroyed, instead of describing the removal of a recipient. (#107)
- A hub's budget is labelled as what it is: a cap on payments made over the hub's own connection, not a limit on how much cash it can mint. (#107)
- Finish after creating a Cash Hub lands on the new hub rather than the Sub-wallets list, which does not list hubs. (#107)
- The title chip names what it switches between — other Cash Hubs, a hub's own bills — instead of calling every grouping a connection. (#107)
